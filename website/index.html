CalmMouse

A small menu-bar app for the Magic Mouse on macOS.

It stops the page from scrolling every time you click.

Download for macOS View on GitHub ›

Free. MIT-licensed. Requires macOS 13 or later.

 

Every click is also
a tiny swipe.

The Magic Mouse's entire top shell is a touch surface — and it stays live while you click. You press down, your finger rolls half a millimetre, and the page jumps. Drag a window and the document scrolls out from under it. Click a link and the link moves first.

macOS has no setting for this. CalmMouse is that setting. It ignores Magic Mouse scrolling while you're clicking, and for a split second after, so the page stays put.

Everything else
it fixes.

Most of it is for the Magic Mouse. CalmMouse knows which device every event comes from, so your trackpad is never touched, and every mouse keeps its own settings.

Tap to click

Tap the surface lightly instead of pressing the mouse down. A tap on the right side can right-click. Taps while you scroll or grip the mouse don't count.

Drag without holding the button

Tap twice and move, or rest two fingers and hold. CalmMouse keeps the button pressed for you and lets go when you lift your finger.

Start from a preset

Pick Just fix clicking, Extra steady, or Trackpad feel and you're done in one click. Change anything afterwards: a preset is a starting point, not a mode.

Every mouse, its own settings

Pick a mouse in Settings and change just that one. A wheel mouse can scroll smoothly, in its own direction and at its own speed. All off until you turn them on.

Reverse just the Magic Mouse

macOS has one natural-scrolling switch for the trackpad and every mouse. This flips only the Magic Mouse, so the trackpad stays the way you like it.

Different rules per app

Turn scrolling off in Figma, or block sideways scroll in Excel. Settings switch with whichever app is in front.

Scroll in straight lines

Scrolling stays vertical or horizontal, never diagonal.

Ignore tiny nudges

A finger resting on the mouse can't move the page.

Stop the glide

Turn off coasting after a flick, on this mouse only.

Scroll with a key held

⌘, ⌥, ⌃, or ⇧ plus scroll can go sideways, invert, or zoom.

Battery warning

A heads-up before any of your mice dies mid-afternoon.

Tap zone

Only the front of the surface accepts taps, so gripping fingers can't click.

The first launch walks you through setup once, and shows the accidental scrolls it just caught while you try it. After that, CalmMouse stays out of the way in the menu bar. If another mouse app, such as Mac Mouse Fix or LinearMouse, changes the same thing, Settings tells you.

You won't need a manual.

Every setting is written in normal words. Point at one and the Magic Mouse in Settings acts it out on its own lid, with a small page underneath showing what happens on screen. If you'd rather not touch settings at all, pick a preset and you're done.

Nothing leaves your Mac.

CalmMouse asks for one macOS permission, called Accessibility. That's what lets an app see and adjust mouse input, and it's all CalmMouse uses it for.

  • No keystrokes It never watches the keyboard or what you type.
  • Only update checks It asks GitHub whether there's a new version, and nothing else. Nothing about you is sent.
  • No collected data It stores nothing except your own settings.
  • Open source The code is public on GitHub, so you can check all of this yourself.

Free. As in actually free.

No trial, no license key, no email. Download it, drag it in, done.

Download CalmMouse for macOS All releases ›

Requires macOS 13 or later, on an Apple silicon or Intel Mac.

  1. 1
    Unzip & drag to Applications.

    You get CalmMouse.app — drop it in /Applications.

  2. 2
    Open it. No warnings.

    Releases are signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple, so CalmMouse opens like any other app.

  3. 3
    Turn on Accessibility access.

    Open CalmMouse and it walks you to the right switch in System Settings. It starts working within two seconds, no relaunch needed.

  • Will it affect my trackpad? No. The trackpad is never touched, and a wheel mouse changes only when you turn on its own settings.
  • What do I need? A Mac on macOS 13 or later, Apple silicon or Intel. It's made for the Magic Mouse, and can smooth out a wheel mouse too.
  • Is it really free? Yes. No trial, no account, and the code is open source.
  • How do updates work? CalmMouse tells you when a new version is out and installs it in one click.

Prefer to build from source? git clone the repo and run ./install.sh.

What's new

CalmMouse keeps itself up to date: when a new version is out, it tells you, and one click in Settings installs it.

  1. 1.4.1 Nov 2026

    A quieter welcome tour

    The devices in the welcome tour fade in where they belong instead of sliding up into place.

  2. 1.4.0 Nov 2026

    A welcome that knows your devices

    The welcome tour now opens with what CalmMouse works with, recognises the mouse or controller you have, and only shows the steps for it. A Magic Mouse gets a short intro. Settings › Devices shows a scroll-wheel mouse and a controller as rendered models you can turn, and each mouse keeps its own colour.

  3. 1.3.0 Nov 2026

    A game controller as the mouse

    Point, click and scroll from the couch. A PS5 or PS4 controller's touchpad works like a laptop's: slide, tap, two fingers to scroll and right-click. On any controller the left stick points, the right stick scrolls, and pressing a stick clicks. Off until you turn it on, and it steps aside while you play a game.

  4. 1.2.2 Nov 2026

    A calmer Settings window

    Switching tabs happens in one step: nothing slides or fades, and the tab buttons stay where they are. Devices opens shorter, with its settings scrolling beside the mouse; drag the window taller or shorter and it remembers. The mouse has more room to turn, and General's sections have headings like the other tabs.

  5. 1.2.1 Nov 2026

    Steadier Settings

    Switching tabs in Settings no longer slides the tab buttons sideways. The Magic Mouse in Devices goes from one demo to the next without jumping: the page under it keeps scrolling instead of snapping back to the top, and each demo fades into the next.

  6. 1.2.0 Nov 2026

    Every mouse, its own settings

    Settings has one Devices page: pick a mouse on the left, change its settings on the right. The Magic Mouse can scroll the other way from the trackpad, every mouse gets a low-battery warning, and CalmMouse now runs on Intel Macs too.

  7. 1.1.3 Nov 2026

    Send feedback in one click

    Send Feedback… in the menu opens a GitHub issue with the details already filled in, listing your mice by hardware ID, never by name. Settings → General has a Support CalmMouse section, and after a week CalmMouse may ask, once, for a star.

  8. 1.1.2 Nov 2026

    Fixes for wheel mice and Mouse type

    Mouse type now corrects a Magic Mouse taken for some other mouse, and tracking speed follows it. Zooming mid-glide no longer zooms too far, and pausing CalmMouse puts each mouse back to its normal speed.

  9. 1.1.1 Nov 2026

    A crash fix, and tracking speed for each mouse

    Fixes a crash when the mice in use changed while Settings was open, and a wheel mouse is now recognized on first launch. Each mouse gets its own tracking speed, the wheel can scroll faster, and Tap and drag is now Double Tap Drag.

  10. 1.1.0 Nov 2026

    Wheel mice, and a simpler Settings

    Connect a mouse with a wheel and CalmMouse can make it glide, reverse its direction on its own, and keep it still while it's pressed. Settings went from seven pages to four, and the Magic Mouse is now recognized even if you renamed it.

  11. 1.0.0 Aug 2026

    One point oh

    The developer scaffolding is gone: no more status file rewritten every two seconds (diagnostics are now produced only when asked for), and the hidden debug switches are retired. What's left is just the app.

  12. 0.6.0 Aug 2026

    CalmMouse speaks Chinese

    The whole app — menu bar, settings, welcome tour — now comes in 简体中文 and 繁體中文, following your Mac's language setting automatically. English stays the default everywhere else.

  13. 0.5.0 Aug 2026

    Tap to right-click, and updates that install themselves

    Tapping learns the right button: a tap on the right side of the surface right-clicks — the same side a physical right click uses — or switch it to a double-tap instead. The Trackpad feel preset now includes it. And CalmMouse now tells you when a new version is out and installs it from inside the app.

  14. 0.4.0 Aug 2026

    Presets and a welcome tour

    Three one-click starting points — Just fix clicking, Extra steady, Trackpad feel — plus your own saved setups, and a first-launch tour that walks through the permission, a preset, and a live try-out.

  15. 0.3.0 Aug 2026

    Godmouse becomes CalmMouse

    New name, new icon, settings rewritten in plain English with animated hover previews — and the whole "scrolling stops working" family of bugs fixed.

  16. 0.2.0 Aug 2026

    Gestures on the surface

    Tap to click, Double Tap Drag, two-finger drag, and a tap zone that keeps gripping fingers from clicking. All off by default.

  17. 0.1.0 Aug 2026

    The headline fix

    No more scrolling while you click — plus dead zone, axis lock, momentum control, per-app rules and modifier shortcuts.
