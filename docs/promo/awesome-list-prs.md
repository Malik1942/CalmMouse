> ## Description
> CalmMouse is a free, MIT-licensed menu bar app for macOS. The Magic Mouse's top is a touch surface that stays live while you click, so every click also scrolls the page a little; CalmMouse blocks scrolling while a button is down and leaves everything else alone. It also adds straight-line scrolling, a momentum switch for the Magic Mouse alone, tap to click and per-app rules, smooths scrolling on wheel mice, and lets a game controller drive the pointer.
>

> Native Swift, signed and notarized, English and Chinese UI, actively maintained (1.4.0 this week). Disclosure: I'm the developer.
